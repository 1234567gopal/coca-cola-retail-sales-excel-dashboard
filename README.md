# Coca-Cola Retail Sales Excel Dashboard

An Excel dashboard that analyses Coca-Cola retail sales using pivot tables and charts to track revenue and top-performing products.

## Problem
Track how Coca-Cola products perform across [regions / retailers / time periods] and identify the biggest revenue drivers.

## Dataset
- Source: Examplary dataset of Coca-Cola found from Genuine Website
- Key columns: Date, Product, Region, Units Sold, Revenue

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Formulas

## What I Did
- Cleaned the raw data (removed duplicates, fixed formats and missing values)
- Built pivot tables to summarise sales by [product, region, month]
- Created charts and KPI cards for total revenue, units sold and top products
- Added slicers so the dashboard can be filtered interactively

## Key Findings
- Sales growth

	1. Sales rose from $2.42M in 2022 to $9.59M in 2023, about 4x.
	2. The dip in Q4 2022 ($368K, roughly half of the earlier quarters) and the Q3 2023 peak ($2.81M) are the two extremes in the quarterly trend.
	3.December 2023 and July 2023 were the strongest months, at about $1.03M each.

- Brands

	1. Coca-Cola is the top brand with $2.77M (23% of sales) and the best margin among the big sellers (42%).
	2. Dasani Water is second ($2.39M, 20%). Diet Coke is third ($2.06M).
	3. Diet Coke has the lowest margin at 36.1%, despite ranking third in sales.
	4. Fanta is the smallest brand (11.9% of sales).

- Regions

	1. The West leads with $3.64M (30% of sales), but it has the lowest margin (35.7%).
	2. The South is only 17% of sales but has the highest margin (44.8%), so it is the most profitable per dollar.
	3. The Midwest is the smallest region (14%).
	4. Dasani in the West earns just a 34.5% margin, versus 53.9% in the South.

- Retailers

	1. West Soda is the largest retailer (27% of sales), followed by BevCo (23%) and FizzyCo (20%).
	2. FizzyCo has the best margin at 43.3%.
	3. Walmart is the smallest at 7.7% of sales.
	4. Target and Amazon both grew strongly in 2023, but from a very small base.

- States

	1. New York, California, Florida and Texas are the top 4, each producing $0.66M to $0.87M.
	2. Washington and Hawaii have the highest prices per unit but the lowest margins (about 28.5%).
	3. Alabama (54.4%) and Tennessee (49.5%) have the best margins.
